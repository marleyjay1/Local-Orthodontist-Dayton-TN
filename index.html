Orthodontic Treatments for Children, Teens, and Adults

Orthodontic treatment is not limited to teenagers. Children, adolescents, and adults can all benefit from an orthodontic evaluation. Identifying alignment problems early can help determine whether treatment is needed immediately or whether monitoring is more appropriate.

Common orthodontic concerns include:

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Gaps and spacing between teeth
  • Overbites and underbites
  • Crossbites and open bites
  • Improper jaw alignment
  • Difficulty maintaining proper oral hygiene due to crowded teeth

Depending on the patient's condition, an orthodontist may recommend traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, or other suitable orthodontic solutions. The right treatment depends on the complexity of the case, the patient's age, and their individual preferences.

Traditional Braces for Effective Teeth Alignment

Traditional braces remain a popular orthodontic treatment for correcting a wide range of alignment and bite problems. They use brackets and wires to apply controlled pressure, gradually moving teeth into their desired positions.

Braces can help address complicated orthodontic issues that affect both the appearance and function of a smile. Regular adjustments allow the orthodontic team to monitor progress and make necessary changes throughout treatment.

For patients in Dayton, TN, discussing available braces options with a qualified orthodontic provider is a useful way to understand treatment expectations, estimated timelines, and ongoing care requirements.

What to Expect During an Orthodontic Consultation

An initial orthodontic consultation is an opportunity to understand your dental alignment and explore suitable treatment options. The orthodontic provider typically evaluates the teeth, bite, and jaw relationship. Diagnostic records, such as photographs, digital scans, or X-rays, may also be recommended when appropriate.

After the evaluation, the provider can explain potential treatment options, estimated treatment duration, appointment frequency, and financial considerations.

Patients should also ask about retainers and post-treatment care. Retainers play an important role in maintaining teeth alignment after braces or other orthodontic treatment has been completed.
